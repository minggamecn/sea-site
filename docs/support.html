Support

Questions, bug reports or ideas about A Thousand Sails? Write to wdswmz@gmail.com. Please include your device model, its Android or iOS version, and the game's version (shown under About on the title screen).

Frequently asked questions

How do I get the full game back on a new device, or after reinstalling?

The full game belongs to the Google Account or Apple Account that bought it, not to the device. Install the Game while signed in to Google Play or the App Store with that same account, then open the Game's Settings and tap Restore purchases. There is nothing to pay again.

A purchase made on Google Play works on Android devices, and one made on the App Store works on iPhone and iPad. Because the Game has no account of its own, a purchase cannot be moved from one store to the other.

Where are my saves kept?

Only on your device. There are no online saves, so we cannot see, recover or move them for you. They can come back through your device's own backup:

  • Android: if backup is turned on, Android may include the Game's saves in your Google Account backup, and they come back when you set up a new phone from it.
  • iPhone and iPad: iCloud Backup, or a backup on your computer, may include the Game's saves, and they come back when you set up a new device from that backup.

If you delete the Game without such a backup, its saves are gone for good. See Delete your data for what is stored where.

How do I ask for a refund?

Payments are handled by the store, not by us, so refunds are too.

  • Google Play: see Google Play's refund help, or start from your order history.
  • App Store: sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com and choose Request a refund; see Apple's refund help.

Which languages does the Game have?

English, Simplified Chinese (简体中文) and Japanese (日本語). You can change the language in the Game's Settings.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. A Thousand Sails is a single-player game that runs fully offline, with no sign-in, no account and no ads. You need a connection only to download the Game and to buy or restore the full game through the store.
