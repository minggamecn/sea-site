Delete your data

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from A Thousand Sails (package name and bundle ID com.mz.wlqf), made by ming zhu, on Android, iPhone and iPad.

There is no account to delete. The Game has no sign-in and no user accounts, and we do not collect or store any personal data on servers. Everything the Game keeps is on your own device, and you can delete it yourself as described below.

Delete a single save

  1. On the title screen, tap Continue.
  2. Choose the save you want to remove.
  3. Tap Delete and confirm.

Delete all of the Game's data

Each of these removes every save and setting from your device.

Android

  • Clear storage: open your phone's Settings → Apps → A Thousand Sails → Storage and tap Clear storage (on some phones, Clear data).
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the Game deletes all of its data from the device.

iPhone and iPad

  • Delete the app: touch and hold the Game's icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. Or open Settings → General → iPhone Storage (iPad Storage) → A Thousand Sails and tap Delete App.
  • Offload App is not enough: it removes the app but keeps its saves on the device.

Delete the device backup

Android. If backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy of the Game's saves in your Google Account so that they come back on a new phone. Google stores this backup, not us. To delete it, open the backups in Google Drive, or on your phone go to Settings → Google → Backup and manage your backups there. You can also turn off backup for your device.

iPhone and iPad. If you use iCloud Backup, your device backup in iCloud may include the Game's saves. Apple stores this backup, not us. To remove the Game from it, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups (on older versions, Manage Storage), choose your device and turn off the Game, or delete the whole backup there. You can also turn off iCloud Backup. A backup you made on a computer is kept on that computer, and you can delete it there.

Purchases

Records of your purchases are kept by the store in your account, not by us: by Google in your Google Account, or by Apple in your Apple Account. Deleting the Game's data does not remove a purchase: the full game unlocks again when you reinstall with the same account (if it does not, tap Restore purchases in the Game's Settings).

  • Android: for your purchase history or a refund, see your Google Play order history and Google Play's refund help.
  • iPhone and iPad: your purchase history is in Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases and at reportaproblem.apple.com; for a refund, see Apple's refund help.

Emails you sent us

If you have written to us and want that correspondence deleted, email wdswmz@gmail.com from the same address with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.
