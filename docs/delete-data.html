Delete your data

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from A Thousand Sails (package com.mz.wlqf), made by ming zhu.

There is no account to delete. The Game has no sign-in and no user accounts, and we do not collect or store any personal data on servers. Everything the Game keeps is on your own device, and you can delete it yourself as described below.

Delete a single save

  1. On the title screen, tap Continue.
  2. Choose the save you want to remove.
  3. Tap Delete and confirm.

Delete all of the Game's data

Either of these removes every save and setting from your device:

  • Clear storage: open your phone's Settings → Apps → A Thousand Sails → Storage and tap Clear storage (on some phones, Clear data).
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the Game deletes all of its data from the device.

Delete the device backup

If backup is turned on, Android may keep a copy of the Game's saves in your Google Account so that they come back on a new phone. Google stores this backup, not us. To delete it, open the backups in Google Drive, or on your phone go to Settings → Google → Backup and manage your backups there. You can also turn off backup for your device.

Purchases

Records of your purchases are kept by Google in your Google Account, not by us. Deleting the Game's data does not remove a purchase: the full game unlocks again when you reinstall with the same account. For your purchase history or a refund, see your Google Play order history and Google Play's refund help.

Emails you sent us

If you have written to us and want that correspondence deleted, email wdswmz@gmail.com from the same address with the subject "Delete my data". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.
