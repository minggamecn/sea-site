A Thousand Sails

Sail, trade and explore the world of 1577

It is 1577. From Lisbon to Nagasaki, lead your fleet across the seas to trade, explore and fight.

Coming soon to Google Play Android · English, 简体中文, 日本語

  • Trade

    Buy low and sell high across 116 ports, from the Baltic to the Spice Islands. Every market has its own goods, gluts and shortages.

  • Explore

    Chart unknown coasts and search out nearly two hundred discoveries: monuments, natural wonders, animals, plants and legends of the wider world.

  • Fight

    Chase merchant fleets, fend off pirates and take on rival captains in sea battles, ship against ship.

  • Command

    Buy and refit ships, hire officers and build a fleet that can cross any ocean.

  • Story and free mode

    Follow a captain's story across the seas, or set out from a great port of your choice with no ending at all.

  • Play anywhere

    A single-player game that runs fully offline. No account, no ads and no data collected.

Free to start. Sail the whole of Europe's seas for free, with no time limit. One purchase opens the rest of the world.
