Privacy Policy

{{updated_label}}: {{updated_long}}

This policy covers the game {{app}} (the "Game", package {{app_id}}) and this website. The Game is made and published by {{developer}} ("we", "us"). If you have a question about it, write to {{email}}.

In short

  • We do not collect, store or share any personal data.
  • The Game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The Game does not use the internet. Its release version does not ask for network access at all.
  • Your saves stay on your phone. Purchases are handled by Google Play.

1. Data the Game keeps on your device

The Game stores your saved games and your settings (such as language and music) in its private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device through the Game, and we cannot see it. You can delete it at any time; see Delete your data.

2. Device backup

If you have turned on backup on your Android device, Android may include the Game's saves in your device backup, so they come back when you set up a new phone. This backup is made and stored by Google in your Google Account under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive or have access to it, and you can turn it off in your device's settings.

3. Purchases

The Game can be started for free, and part of it is unlocked by an in-app purchase. Purchases are made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we never receive your name, email address, card or other payment details. Google Play tells the Game on your device which products your Google Account owns, so that the Game can unlock them. The Game does not send this information anywhere. Google's handling of purchases is described in Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

4. Information from Google Play

As the publisher, Google Play gives us aggregated reports, such as the number of installs, ratings and reviews you choose to post, and crash and performance statistics from users who have agreed to share diagnostics with Google. These reports do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems and improve the Game.

5. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you report. We do not add you to any mailing list, and we delete the correspondence when it is no longer needed. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

6. This website

This website is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. GitHub, as the host, may log technical information such as IP addresses to keep the service secure; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about players, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have written to us, you may ask for a copy of that correspondence or for its deletion, and we will do so promptly. You also have the rights given to you by the laws of your country, such as the GDPR in the European Union or the UK.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the Game changes what is described here, for example by adding an online feature, we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the Game's release notes.
